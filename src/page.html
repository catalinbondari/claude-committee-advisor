How it works

  1. 1

    Load

    Upload ISINs and weights, or pick holdings from a list. Set your risk, horizon and a cap on any one company.

  2. 2

    Members propose

    Nine members, including a disaster sceptic, each propose from one distinct lens.

  3. 3

    Rate

    Each scores the others' ideas from −5 to +5. A wide spread marks a contested idea.

  4. 4

    Rule

    The CIO approves up to three. House rules check the money adds up, then each order gets a limit and an expiry.

What each column means
isin
The 12-character ISIN on your broker's page or the factsheet, e.g. IE00BK5BQT80.
weight
% of the portfolio: 40 means 40%. A % sign is fine.
price
Optional. Latest price per share in euro, for an exact limit price.
CASH
The weight of cash you have to invest.
